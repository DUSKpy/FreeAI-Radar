{# themecontrols.html -- appearance switches. Four independent choices, each persisted separately: * theme light / dark / follow system * backdrop solid colour / photo * glass tint 0 (ultra-clear) .. 100 (fully opaque), stepless * motion animated / reduced The tint is a slider rather than the old two-way switch. iOS 26.1 shipped Clear/Tinted as two states and iOS 27 replaced it with a continuous slider, because "how much glass is right" has no single answer: with two states, anyone who finds the glass slightly too clear can only give it up entirely. They are deliberately separate controls rather than one "accessibility" master switch, because a user may want the glass look but no motion. The backdrop control carries visible text rather than the icon-only + sr-only pattern the theme control uses. Three sun/moon glyphs are self-explanatory; two abstract swatches are not, and the whole point of the photo backdrop is that it is discoverable. #}
{# Glass tint -- a continuous slider, not a two-way switch. iOS 26.1 shipped Clear/Tinted as two states; iOS 27 replaced it with a stepless slider from ultra-clear to fully tinted. The reason matters more than the shape of the control: "how much glass is right" has no single correct answer, so a binary switch forces anyone who finds the glass slightly too clear to give up the material entirely. range rather than checkbox: the value is a quantity and the control should say so. aria-valuetext carries the named ends because "64" means nothing to a screen-reader user while "偏实色" does. #}

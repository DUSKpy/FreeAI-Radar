{# themecontrols.html -- appearance switches. Four independent choices, each persisted separately: * theme light / dark / follow system * backdrop solid colour / photo * transparency full glass / reduced * motion animated / reduced They are deliberately separate controls rather than one "accessibility" master switch, because a user may want the glass look but no motion. The backdrop control carries visible text rather than the icon-only + sr-only pattern the theme control uses. Three sun/moon glyphs are self-explanatory; two abstract swatches are not, and the whole point of the photo backdrop is that it is discoverable. #}

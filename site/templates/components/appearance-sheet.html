{# appearance-sheet.html -- the appearance controls, reachable on a phone. Why this exists --------------- The theme, glass-tint and motion controls live in the sidebar and in the tablet top navigation. On a phone neither of those renders -- the sidebar is hidden below 1024px and the topnav is hidden below 768px in favour of the bottom tab bar. The result was that a phone user had NO way to switch to dark mode, tint the glass, or reduce motion. That is a real defect, not a cosmetic one: being able to make the glass opaque is an accessibility control, not a preference. Rather than un-hiding a control somewhere it does not fit, this is a sheet opened from the tab bar. It reuses the same markup and the same data attributes as the sidebar copy, so theme.js wires it up automatically -- it selects by attribute, not by position. It is always in the DOM but only reachable from the phone tab bar, which is the only place it is needed. #}

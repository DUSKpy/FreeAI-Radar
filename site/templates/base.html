{# The refraction filter, referenced from glass.css by url(#radar-refract). Why it is inline and not a separate .svg file: a filter referenced across documents is not honoured by any current browser, and one referenced from shadow DOM is not either. It has to be in this document. Why width/height are 0 and not display:none: a filter whose element is display:none is never resolved, so backdrop-filter: url() would silently do nothing. The filter is built as a real optical chain rather than a single blur: feTurbulence at a low baseFrequency + feGaussianBlur -> a smooth, non-repeating displacement field. A PNG would have to be downloaded and would visibly tile; this costs nothing and never repeats. feDisplacementMap -> bends the backdrop along that field. --glass-refract caps it at 8 because past about 12 the edge stops reading as glass and starts reading as a bug. three feColorMatrix + feDisplacementMap + feBlend -> splits the channels by a fraction of a pixel and recombines them, which is where the faint red/cyan fringe along a hard edge comes from. Every filter primitive reads the SourceGraphic, so nothing accumulates and the result is deterministic rather than depending on evaluation order. Safari and Firefox do not support backdrop-filter: url() at all. They fall back to the plain frosted material in glass.css, which is a complete design in its own right -- see the note at the top of that file. #}
